About AgroScan

We connect Agriculture with modern AI.

AgroScan is an open-source starter template for machine learning-based soil analysis, developed for scientists, agri-tech startups, and farmers.

Mission

Why AgroScan?

"Precision agriculture shouldn’t fail because of technical complexity. Our goal is a template that becomes production-ready in hours — not weeks."

Satellite data, soil sensors, weather services — the data already exists. What’s missing is a simple framework that connects cartographic visualization with a plug-and-play ML backend. AgroScan fills that gap.
